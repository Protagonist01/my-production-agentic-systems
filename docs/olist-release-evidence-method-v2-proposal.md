# Decision proposal: Olist release evidence without a standing reviewer team

Prepared 2026-11-05 from verified main `6ca379d`. **Proposed, not activated.** This is the
concrete substitution-method decision required by section4 of the
[execution plan](automated-review-execution-plan.md). Existing Review kinds, `_reviewed`,
profiles, historical studies and release decisions remain unchanged.

## Decision and preserved requirements

Implement a separate opt-in Olist v2 audit that evaluates source-backed semantic evidence
and independently executable facts directly, rather than manufacturing a blind-human
Review record. The owner accepts the evidence method and operating risk; source applicability
and evaluator results must still establish the individual claims. Acceptance of this design
does not mean any current study passes or that a source's scope has expanded.

Keep all eight required fields and eight holdout slices from `olist-evaluation-standard-v1`.
Keep the accepted planning targets: zero observed critical effects; at least95% joint
intent/amount agreement, eligible completion and required-review routing; p95 API1second /
model decision30seconds; availability99.5% over30days; host-loss RPO15minutes / RTO60minutes.
Freeze the actual statistical design, group/slice floors, uncertainty/error limits,
abstention limits and call/token/cost bounds before qualification capture. Unset design
requirements block; this proposal invents no universal72-family or434-call requirement.

## New records, no disguised human review

The new path would bind an accepted method-policy pin to per-claim evidence records:
criterion, exact input/reference hashes, method, source/evaluator qualification and allowed
scope, supported/contradicted/unresolved disposition, actual observation and provenance.
External-reference and construction applicability must be established for the claim being
made; a generic source URL or qualification-report pointer cannot stand in for verification.

A semantic model evaluator must have an actual, separately frozen qualification report
covering its field/slices with externally supported references, complete decided/unresolved
denominators and reviewed uncertainty. Different prompts/providers do not establish
independence. No current Olist semantic judge is qualified by this proposal.

The v2 audit must refuse missing/changed evidence, unsupported scope, failed invariants,
unknown required accounting, incomplete schedules, post-output protocol changes and
unqualified semantic decisions. Contradiction or an unsafe/unknown critical observation
blocks regardless of aggregate score. Baseline and threshold approvals are separately
recorded owner decisions before the candidate interval; they are not label truth or
blind-review identities. An unaccepted policy cannot replace any legacy requirement.

Any v1 audit continues to apply its original rules. Old failures remain failures under
their original source/profile/policy versions. No skip_review option, new human identity,
automatic model-judge activation or retrospective relabelling is part of this proposal.

## Worked examples from the accepted development package

**Payment100.00SIM, request25.00SIM.** The controlled reference explicitly declares the
two money roles. An independent numeric check establishes2500requested minor units for
that accepted constructed sentence. The SIM table rejects a partial refund; actual SQL
can prove zero effect and truthful review. These facts do not qualify unrelated natural
wording, and current development labels cannot be renamed into holdout.

**Clarification misread as refund.** If the model-like decision is refund but the original
reference is clarification, semantic correctness fails. Withholding customer consent may
prevent an effect and pass an authority/state-safety check; it cannot fix that prediction.
If a separate scripted customer confirms an exact refund and it commits, authority may
be valid while the reference-relative action/state contract fails. Never erase either fact.
